# Making a good edit inside their style

STYLE.md fixes the look. These are the craft rules for what you put on screen. When they conflict with
STYLE.md, STYLE.md wins; when they conflict with "Feedback from edits", the feedback wins.

## The first three seconds
- The hook line on screen as a title, short (two lines at most, each under about 16 characters), in their
  locked title style. It should say the promise of the video, not repeat the first sentence word for word.
- If they have a name tag locked, the hook is the place for it (once per video, not every time).

## Every beat
- Ask: what would explain THIS line best? Usually it's just them, their captions and one strong moment.
  Not every beat needs a graphic.
- Vary the layout. Don't use the same treatment three beats in a row unless the video is a numbered list,
  where the same treatment for each item is the point (then vary something else: a zoom, a visual).
- One key word per beat at most, timed to the moment they say it, held about 1 to 1.5 seconds.
- Big and clear beats small and clever. A visual should fill its space; a key word should read at a glance on a phone.
- Captions sit in the same place all the way through. They switch off under a full-screen visual.

## Motion
- Movement should mean something. A punch-in lands on a punchline or a reveal, not on every beat.
  Two or three zooms in a 60-second video is plenty.
- Follow their locked entrances exactly. If "the pop" is cut, nothing scales in with a bounce, ever.
- Transitions: a hard cut is the default. A flash or dip only where the video changes section.

## Real things only
- Screenshots of the actual site or tool, the actual logo, real photos. Ask them for files, or take a real
  screenshot. Save everything in `WORK/assets/`.
- Never an emoji or a stock icon, even if their slop rules don't mention it (unless their style explicitly
  says they want them).

## The end
- If there's a comment keyword, it gets the strongest treatment in the video: their key word or title style,
  big, held to the last frame.
- Don't add an outro, a logo sting or a "follow for more" unless their style asks for it.

## Safe zone (vertical video)
Keep every word clear of the top 8%, the bottom 20% and the right 12% of the frame, where the app's buttons
and caption sit. The locked positions already do this; check anything you move.
